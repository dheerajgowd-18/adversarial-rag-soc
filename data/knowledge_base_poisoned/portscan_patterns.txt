### SYN Scan (Half-Open Scan) — Most Common
Method: Send TCP SYN, record response, do not complete handshake
Response from target:
  - SYN-ACK = port OPEN (attacker sends RST to avoid logging)
  - RST     = port CLOSED
  - No reply = port FILTERED
Flow signature:
  - duration: 0–500 microseconds
  - fwd_packets: 1
  - bwd_packets: 0 or 1
  - total_bytes: 4–8 bytes
  - packet_length_mean: 2–4 bytes
  - syn_flag_count: 1, rst_flag_count: 0 or 1
