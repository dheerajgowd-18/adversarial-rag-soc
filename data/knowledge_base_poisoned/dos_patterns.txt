target resources. Unlike DDoS (distributed), DoS flows often have more distinguishable
per-flow signatures because the attack volume comes from one machine.

### DoS Hulk
Target: Web servers (port 80/443)
Method: Generates unique HTTP GET requests per connection to bypass caching
Signature:

  - Each flow: 6-12 packets, ~12KB total
  - flow_bytes_per_sec: varies widely (10–5,300,000 B/s)
  - packet_length_mean: ~800 bytes (large, mimics real HTTP)
  - Long flow duration: 7–120 seconds
