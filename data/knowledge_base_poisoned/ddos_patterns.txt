## Overview
Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks flood a target with traffic from multiple
sources simultaneously, overwhelming it until it cannot serve legitimate users. DDoS is
among the hardest attack types to detect because individual flows look statistically
normal — the threat is in volume across many sources, not in any single flow.
